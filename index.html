Listening to the Spotify desktop app
  • Windows / ChromeOS: tap Listen to Spotify → Entire screen → switch on Share system audio.
  • Mac (Chrome 141 or newer): tap Listen to Spotify → Window → pick the Spotify window, with audio on. Older Macs: use Mic.
  • Spotify in a browser tab: pick the tab → Share tab audio.
  • No screen sharing (Windows): turn on Stereo Mix in Sound settings → Recording, then tap Mic and pick Stereo Mix from the input list.
  • New songs: Sipra notices the gap between songs and starts fresh, saving the last song to Stats. For songs that run straight into each other, tap New song.

    01

    Periodicity

    The beat repeats every T seconds, so beat k lands at t = k·T.

    T = 60 / BPM  ·  N = D · BPM / 60

    Start listening to see it worked out with your song.

    Tap a tempo →
    02

    Equal temperament

    Each semitone multiplies the frequency by 21/12: a geometric sequence. Twelve steps double it.

    f = 440 · 2n/12

    Start listening to see it worked out with your song.

    Play the keyboard →
    03

    Ratios in chords

    Chord notes sound smooth together because their frequencies are close to small whole-number ratios.

    major ≈ 4 : 5 : 6  ·  minor ≈ 10 : 12 : 15

    Start listening to see it worked out with your song.

    Hear the ratios →
    04

    Bars, loops & remainders

    Counting beats in a bar is division with remainder; a chord loop is a periodic sequence.

    beat in bar = (k mod B) + 1  ·  loop = L · B · T

    Start listening to see it worked out with your song.

    Try polyrhythms →
    05

    Fourier analysis

    Any sound is a sum of sine waves. The spectrum shows how strong each frequency is.

    Xk = Σ xn e−i2πkn/N

    Start listening to see it worked out with your song.
