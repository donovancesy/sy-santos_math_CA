The concepts

01Periodicity & beat spacing

Music as a periodic function

T = 60 / BPM  ·  N = D · BPM / 60

One beat every T seconds; beat k sits at t = kT. Tap along to a song and the period is the average gap between taps.

02Geometric sequences

Equal temperament

fn = 440 · 2n/12  ·  r = 21/12 ≈ 1.05946

Each semitone multiplies the frequency by the same ratio. Twelve steps give ×2: one octave.

Pitch lab →
03Ratios & fractions

Why chords sound smooth

major ≈ 4 : 5 : 6  ·  fifth ≈ 3 : 2

Notes whose frequencies are close to small whole-number ratios blend; the keyboard shows how close equal temperament gets.

Pitch lab →
04Fourier analysis

One signal, many frequencies

Xk = Σn=0N−1 xn e−i2πkn/N

Any sound is a sum of sine waves. The FFT view shows how much of each frequency a song has.

Signal lab →
05LCM & modular arithmetic

Polyrhythms and bars

LCM(3, 4) = 12  ·  LCM(x, y) = xy / GCD(x, y)

3 against 4 lines up every 12 steps. Counting beats in a bar is k mod B.

Rhythm lab →
06Logarithms

Loudness in decibels

L = 20 · log10(A / Aref)

Hearing works in ratios, so loudness uses a log scale: doubling the amplitude adds about 6 dB.

See it live →
07Descriptive statistics

Summarizing a listening session

mean = Σx / n  ·  range = max − min

Mean, median, mode, range and spread: five views of the tempos you listened to.

08Optimization

How the chord chart is written

best path = max Σ (fit − cost of change)

Each beat gets a score for every chord; dynamic programming (Viterbi) finds the best path through the song.
